MiHa  /  Our Story

Two families,one table.

Fifteen years ago, the Miya and Hassim families became one. MiHa is a celebration of that union, and of the table it built.

01 The name

MiMiya
HaHassim

Said together, MiHa is also a term of endearment, which is exactly how we want every guest to feel: welcomed, fed well and part of the family.

02 What we believe

Three promises, every visit.

Strictly Halaal

Every dish from every MiHa kitchen is strictly Halaal. No exceptions, no need to ask.

Alcohol-free, always

For religious reasons no alcohol is served or allowed, so the whole family can share one table.

Made around you

A quick grab-and-go or a decadent celebratory feast: the simple and the sumptuous, under one roof.

04 The journey

How MiHa grew.

  1. Chapter I

    Two families become one

    The Miya and Hassim families come together, and the name MiHa is born.

  2. Chapter II

    Melrose Arch

    MiHa opens on the piazza at Melrose Arch, in the heart of Johannesburg.

  3. Chapter III

    Centurion

    A second home opens at Golden Point Padel on Baard Road, Raslouw.

  4. Chapter IV

    Clenergy & catering

    Our own bakery, corporate catering, events and staff lunches take MiHa beyond the table.

  5. Chapter V

    MiHa X Lenasia

    The newest chapter: gourmet food at exceptional value, at Trade Route Mall.
