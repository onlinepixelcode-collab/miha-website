MiHa  /  Our Story

Two families,one table.

Fifteen years ago, the Miya and Hassim families became one. The name MiHa is a celebration of that union.

01 The name

Mi for Miya. Ha for Hassim. Said together, MiHa is also a term of endearment, which is exactly how we want every guest to feel: welcomed, fed well and part of the family.

02 The idea

The simple & the sumptuous.

MiHa is the coming together of a casual café and a classic dining experience. From a quick grab-and-go to a decadent celebratory feast, we bring the two together under one roof.

Our menu is international, with flavours from near and far and our own South African touch.

03 Our promise

Halaal, always.

All food at MiHa is strictly Halaal. For religious reasons, no alcohol is served or allowed on the premises, so our bar pours mocktails, shakes, fresh juices and coffee instead.

It means everyone can sit at the same table, and nobody has to ask.
