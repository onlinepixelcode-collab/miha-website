01 Solo

Stack the Burger.

How to play

  1. Tap Start stacking.
  2. Each layer swings across the burger. Tap the board (or press space) to drop it.
  3. Anything hanging over the edge falls off, so line it up. A perfect drop keeps your burger wide.
  4. Miss the burger completely and it’s game over.

02 1–4 players

MiHa Memory.

How to play

  1. Choose how many players.
  2. On your turn, flip two cards.
  3. Same dish? Keep the pair and go again. Different? Next player’s turn.
  4. Most pairs wins. Playing solo? Find all eight in as few moves as you can.

03 2 players or vs MiHa

Mi vs Ha.

How to play

  1. Play a friend at the table, or take on MiHa.
  2. Mi goes first, then Ha. Take turns tapping a square.
  3. Get three in a row (across, down or diagonal) to win the round.
  4. The scoreboard keeps count. Losers go first next round.

04 Solo · 30 seconds

Tap the Dish.

How to play

  1. Tap Start. You have 30 seconds.
  2. Dishes pop up in the arches. Tap each one before it disappears: +1.
  3. A golden MiHa plate is worth +3.
  4. Tap an empty arch and you lose a point. It gets faster as you go.

05 1 player or the table

The Food Quiz.

How to play

  1. Ten questions about food from around the world, and a few about MiHa.
  2. Tap the answer you think is right. You’ll see straight away if you got it.
  3. Playing as a table? Read the question out loud and shout your answers before anyone taps.
  4. Every game picks new questions, so play again.

06 The whole table

Would you rather?

How to play

  1. Tap Draw a card and read it out.
  2. Everyone picks a side and says why.
  3. Draw the next card whenever you’re ready. There are no wrong answers.

07 The whole table

Who’s choosing dessert?

How to play

  1. Everyone puts one finger on the circle.
  2. Hold still. After a moment the LED ring spins.
  3. The finger left glowing chooses dessert for the table.
